# Official ICH quality sources

Selected reference set; document identities and cited sections inspected on
2026-09-08. Dates below are **FDA issuance dates**, not ICH adoption dates.
All links point to FDA-hosted final ICH guidance documents. An ICH document hosted
by FDA remains distinct from separately issued FDA-specific guidance.

Use with [Control-strategy guidance](control-strategy-guidance.md). This index
is not an exhaustive applicability assessment, a full-text archive, or a claim
that every listed document applies. Section numbers use the ICH numbering printed
in the PDFs; Q8's parent and annex have separate numbering.

## Verification and maintenance

- Before relying on exact wording, a numerical expectation, or a regulatory
  implication, inspect the relevant official section or an identified official
  local copy. A document title or this index alone cannot establish it.
- Check the issuing authority for revisions, final/draft status, and regional
  implementation when current applicability matters. A download URL may serve
  updated content; identify the edition actually read.
- If the text is unavailable, distinguish the maintained local synthesis from
  verified source wording. Continue supported scientific reasoning; identify
  the specific conclusion that needs source verification.
- When maintaining this set, record the inspected version/date and revise
  dependent reasoning notes. Do not rewrite the historical basis of a saved
  assessment to imply it used a newer edition.
- Add other applicable ICH documents or separately labeled FDA-specific sources
  when the question requires them. Their omission here is not evidence that no
  guidance exists.
