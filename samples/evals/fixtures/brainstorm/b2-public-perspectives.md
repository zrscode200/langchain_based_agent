# B2 — Synthetic program Linden

Scientist request: "Brainstorm ways to rethink this handling-related signal loss.
Could another field's approach help us see something we are missing? Use the
available public perspectives if they help develop or challenge an idea."

Scenario premises:
- Measured protein signal is lower after contact with one container type than
  another. Container, recovery and measurement effects remain unresolved.
- Both groups use the same assay. No structural measurement or material balance
  has been supplied. Conditions are near-neutral, with no freezing step.
- A simulated public search has already supplied the following two cards.

Paper A: an abstract reports recovery of protein signal using an intervention
after exposure to a surface, with an attractive claim of general usefulness.
Its methods, tables and applicability conditions are not yet in context.

Paper B: a methods abstract from another scientific field describes separating
sample-recovery behavior from instrument response through independent controls.
It does not study this product or container.

These are invented source claims, not verified program mechanisms. Simulated
actions available on request: qualify Paper A or B metadata; inspect an outline,
methods or table; follow references, citing works, or similar articles. State a
specific source action and its purpose if useful; the evaluator will supply the
corresponding response. Do not make real provider calls or invent their results.
