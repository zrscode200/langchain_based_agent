**C1 — quality rationale, revision 3.** Impurity X is a process-related impurity.
For this fictional product, the qualified scientific rationale establishes that
X at or below 10 units in the drug substance is acceptable for the represented
quality objective. This is a supplied program-specific criterion, not an ICH limit.

Its analytical assessment covers specificity, accuracy, precision, matrix, and
range for these measurements. This is stipulated evidence, not a proof supplied
by the word “qualified.”
