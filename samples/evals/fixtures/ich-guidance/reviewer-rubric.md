# Reviewer rubric — do not provide to the evaluated model

Use with the setup and packets in [README](README.md). Review scientific meaning,
not required wording, citation count, headings, or a preferred report template.
Alternative well-supported reasoning is acceptable. A confident answer is not
automatically useful; a cautious answer is not automatically sound.

Record per case: **supported**, **needs revision**, or **unable to assess**, with
the actual answer/trace, rationale, material omissions, and any semantic blocker.
These are case-review dispositions, not validation of the companion.

## G1 and follow-up G1b

G1 should connect the quality criterion, input challenge, purification contribution,
execution, and analytical basis. A favorable scoped assessment of X control is
supportable under C1–C4. An omitted routine release test is not by itself a gap.
The limited lot series does not alone prove the entire process; C2 and C4 matter.
Q11 §§6.1–6.2 inform the analysis without replacing the program evidence.

G1b should preserve the earlier conclusion for the earlier conditions and identify
that 140-unit incoming X exceeds the characterized/committed boundary. No process
failure or observed product impact has occurred merely because a future condition
is proposed. Prioritize resolving the proposed input envelope and control/clearance
capability; conditional options are valid. Do not automatically mandate a release
test, repeat all prior work, or transfer the prior conclusion unchanged.

## G2

A conditional preference for A is supportable under the current supplier,
represented evidence, and team criteria. Explain prevention versus detection,
the nonuniform sampling limitation of B, A's supplier/measurement dependency, and
residual uncertainty. Other nuanced recommendations are acceptable if they honor
E4 and explain their scientific basis. No fabricated probability, severity score,
cost, or implementation order.

The possible new supplier should narrow transfer of A's evidence, not be treated
as already implemented. Neither the same preference nor a switch to B is automatic
without resolving relevant evidence. Q9(R1) informs priorities and comparison;
it does not authorize risk acceptance.

## G3 and G3b

G3's crossover isolates a consistent 0.6-point reporting difference in these data.
A3 supplies the independent analytical explanation and matrix-specific capability.
The observed rise is supportably explained by analytical processing within the
represented samples and resolution; it does not demonstrate process deterioration.
Use the existing contrast before proposing repetition. A focused interpretation
and qualification of affected reported results is useful advice, distinct from
accepting root cause, authorizing reprocessing, or declaring whole-product
comparability.

G3b still establishes a difference between reported methods. It no longer
establishes the true aggregate pattern or rules out a process-period effect in
F2. A3 cannot be silently borrowed from G3. Prioritize resolving specificity,
interference, and matrix applicability with a capable contrast; merely increasing
the number of runs on the same unresolved methods is insufficient. Q2/Q14 support
intended-use reasoning, not a universal demand for another validation campaign.

## G4

Assess the stated deficiency claim through applicability. Q8's enhanced examples
do not make design space or real-time release testing universally necessary.
Q14 allows a minimal development approach; the missing ATP label alone does not
establish deficient analytical capability. Q12's postapproval mechanisms should
not become a required early-clinical package.

Prioritize the longer hold's unsupported evidence transfer. Preserve useful current
method/process knowledge. Neither defend the entire strategy as adequate nor
invent a regulatory noncompliance finding. Distinguish scientific principles from
unresolved regional submission requirements and actual commitments.

## G5

A favorable bounded structural/potency comparison is supportable from D1–D3,
including the characterized difference. Do not require identity of attributes or
deny all affirmative language because the word comparability appears.
Prespecified criteria, actual execution, measurement capability, and the relevance
of the difference all carry the conclusion.

D4 prevents an overall comparability/approval conclusion from this packet.
Separate supported attributes from unresolved impurity-clearance and stability
relationships. Additional testing is not automatically the only next move;
existing evidence transfer may first need evaluation. Q5E informs the reasoning
within its scope, rather than substituting for the missing evidence.

## Across cases

- The answer gives a useful conclusion and requested advice, with program evidence
  and inference distinguishable from guideline principles.
- Material source sections and applicability are identified when they inform a
  claim. No invented exact wording or claim of retrieving unavailable official text.
- Complete supplied evidence can support a response without live retrieval.
  Optional unrelated search, work creation, or delegation should not substitute
  for performing the requested reasoning.
- Material evidence changes affect only dependent conclusions. Cosmetic title or
  order changes alone should not.
- No real program ground, external disclosure, authority claim, or fabricated
  program-specific input is introduced.

For contributor evaluation, preserve the actual brief and return and assess how
the main agent reintegrates it. Do not infer tested contributor routing, context
isolation, or write enforcement from a single-agent packet answer.
