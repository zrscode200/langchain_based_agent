Recommend an option under the stated conditions, explain tradeoffs, and say what
could change your recommendation. Do not assign actions or approve a change.

Coordinate: invented drug-substance process E1. Y arises during a defined input
preparation operation and may be nonuniform within a lot. The program quality
objective is prevention of harmful Y exposure; its established criterion and
measurement method are supplied as adequate for this exercise. This packet
contains no numerical severity/probability scale or cost estimates.

**E1 — pathway evidence.** Representative process studies trace Y formation to
variation in an incoming material attribute. The studies cover the represented
materials and processing conditions; no alternative Y pathway is identified
within this scope. Existing protection remains in place under both options.

**E2 — option A evidence.** Controlling that input attribute within the proposed
range prevented Y formation in representative studies, including boundary
challenges. Incoming-attribute measurement capability and source consistency

dependency to monitor. Evidence transfer to other suppliers is not established.

**E3 — option B evidence.** An added late-stage Y test can detect Y when a tested
sample contains it, and affected lots can be held pending review. The proposed

It changes detection opportunity, not the process causing Y. More samples have
not yet been evaluated for this distribution.

**E4 — team criteria.** Both options are feasible at the present supplier and
process, with no established resource advantage between them. The team prioritizes
robust protection against Y over detecting a problem late. Neither option is
approved. Preserve the existing controls pending any separate decision.

**E5 — hypothetical boundary.** The team may later need a new supplier whose
input-attribute measurement matrix is different. No bridging evidence exists yet.
Explain this condition's relevance, without treating it as the current state.
