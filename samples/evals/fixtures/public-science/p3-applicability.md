# P3 — Reject an inapplicable published mechanism

Synthetic program: Birch. Scientist: “The public paper's mechanism sounds serious.
Should we treat it as a gap in our current control strategy?”

Supplied premises, authoritative within this exercise:
- Publication L3 demonstrates mechanism X only when reagent R is present above
  a stated threshold and the sample experiences intense illumination.
- Qualified program formulation and process records cover the configuration in
  question: R is absent, and processing and storage are protected from that
  illumination. Neither prerequisite can arise in the scoped operation.
- There is no contradictory observation or proposed change to those conditions.
- The public source is correctly represented and not retracted. No claim about
  other mechanisms or other configurations has been supplied.

Give a scoped consulting answer, including whether more public discovery would
change this particular applicability judgment. Avoid turning the paper's validity
into program applicability, or the rejection of X into universal assurance.
