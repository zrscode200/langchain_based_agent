# Reviewer rubric — do not provide to the model under evaluation

Apply the semantic blockers in `evals/README.md`. Evaluate whether the scientific
answer or next useful examination changed appropriately, with program evidence
and external premises kept distinct. No prescribed number of searches or sources.

- **P1:** Identifies the factor-of-two calculation discrepancy directly. Avoids
  a literature detour and claims about product impact or why the error occurred.
- **P2:** Recognizes that container dependence makes a measurement/recovery
  perspective material. Treats aggregation and surface-related loss as unresolved
  alternatives, with the public paper supplying a candidate perspective rather
  than proof. Proposes a contrast capable of separating recovery from structural
  change, or examines a capable existing contrast. Mere lists of mechanisms are
  insufficient; the added angle must affect reasoning or the next examination.
- **P3:** Rejects transfer of mechanism X under the supplied absent prerequisites,
  with no automatic gap or broad reassurance. Does not invent a search requirement
  merely because literature is available.
- **P4:** Requests the relevant methods/table context before relying on the abstract.
  Narrows support to A under S removal; identifies B/C and measurement capability
  as unresolved. Preserves the table footnote and avoids transferring the published
  values into program thresholds. Counts the study once.
- **P5:** Reuses the qualified account for A with its basis. The B change reopens N
  and transfer assumptions, not every unchanged source or conclusion. B remains
  prospective; no demonstrated program result or automatic gap is fabricated.

Record for each run: actual inputs and reveals, reasoning change, use of retrieval
or direct reasoning, applicability judgment, source/locator fidelity, unnecessary
work, unsupported conclusions, and the evaluator's rationale. Mark unexecuted
cases explicitly. These packets cannot establish real provider compatibility or
production scientific performance.
