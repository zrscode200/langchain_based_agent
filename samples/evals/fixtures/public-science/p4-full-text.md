# P4 — Methods narrow the abstract

Synthetic program: Elm. Scientist: “This paper reports excellent clearance. Can
we use it to support our proposed operating window?”

Program evidence in this exercise: proposed window spans conditions A through C.
No program clearance measurements are supplied. The method used in the proposed
program study has not yet been qualified for residual interferent S.

Simulated public record L4 abstract: “High clearance was observed across the
conditions studied.” The card gives no definition of the tested conditions.

Available simulated article outline: Methods, Results, Table 1, Discussion.
The evaluator provides requested material from these supplied premises:
- Methods: only condition A was tested; interferent S was removed before analysis.
- Table 1: header “Condition”, “Clearance”; one condition A with three replicates.
  Caption identifies the same measurement method. Footnote: “S removed before
  quantification; measurements do not characterize performance with S present.”
- Discussion: transfer to B or C and performance without S removal were not studied.

Respond with what source reading is needed, what the published result could
support, and what remains unresolved for this program. Treat the source as one
study regardless of how many sections or projections are read.
