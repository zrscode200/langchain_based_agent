# P5 — Reuse qualified exploration, then update its boundary

Synthetic program: Fir. A saved qualified account states:
- Earlier public exploration examined mechanisms M and N under condition A.
  Exact source identities, methods-section locators and retrieval dates are saved.
- Program observations supported the expected signature of M under A. N's
  necessary condition was absent. This account is an inference from named records,
  not an independent measurement.
- Revisit triggers: change from A, new contradictory observations, or evidence
  changing the cited sources' interpretation. No claim of exhaustive mechanisms.

Phase 1: all stated conditions and program records remain unchanged and the source
basis has been checked for suitability to this use. Scientist: “Explain why the
current control addresses the mechanism we identified.”

Phase 2, given only after the first answer: proposed condition B introduces N's
necessary condition, which was absent under A. No new observations under B exist.
Scientist: “Can we carry the same conclusion over to B?”

Keep the two phases separate. Decide what can be reused, what needs examination,
and which parts of the earlier account remain useful. Do not restart all discovery
or treat the saved inference as independent evidence.
